Choose a potato mood and leave a line or a little moment. Everything is optional. You don’t have to write every day.

How are entries saved?

Changes save automatically on your device. “Done for today” opens the reading view. Tap “Edit” to change today’s entry. You can also edit yesterday’s entry before 5 AM local time. Older entries are read-only.

How do I back up my journal?

  1. Open Settings → Export backup and save the ZIP file containing entries and photos in Files or another location you choose.
  2. On another device, open Settings → Import backup and select that file (legacy JSON backups are also supported).

Import adds missing dates and keeps existing entries on the same date. Exported files are not encrypted. Keep them somewhere safe and make a backup before deleting the app or changing devices.

Can I add a photo?

Choose one photo per day from the entry screen. Photos stay on your device.

Why didn’t my reminder arrive?

Enable the daily reminder in Settings → Reminders and allow notifications on your iPhone. It is a silent local reminder at 11 PM in your device’s time zone. Focus modes or Scheduled Summary may delay delivery.

Can you recover my entries?

The developer does not receive or store your journal and cannot recover it. If you have an exported backup, you can import it. Device backups and restores depend on your system settings and the backup provider.

Contact
