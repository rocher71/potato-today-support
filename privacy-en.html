Privacy policy

Potato Today
Effective October 2, 2026

Your journal stays on your device

No account is required. Moods, journal notes, little moments and sleep entries are stored in the app’s storage on your device. They are not sent to a developer-operated server. The app does not use advertising, behavioral analytics or tracking SDKs.

Backups and external services

If you choose to export a backup, the iOS share sheet lets you save or share a JSON file. The exported file is not separately encrypted. The storage location or recipient you choose may have its own privacy policy. Import reads only the backup file you select and keeps existing entries on the same date. Temporary import and export files are deleted when the operation ends.

Retention and deletion

Entries remain on the device until you delete them or the app’s storage is removed. You can delete today’s entry from the editor; past entries are read-only. Deleting the app may remove local entries. You must separately delete any backups from their saved locations. Operating-system device backups and restores follow your system settings and the provider’s policies.

Optional reminders

Reminders are off by default. Permission is requested only when you enable them. The 11 PM reminder is scheduled on your device. We do not collect push tokens or send them to a notification server. Notifications never include journal content. Disable reminders in the app or in iPhone Settings.

Support inquiries

If you email us, your reply address and the information in your message are used to respond to your inquiry. You do not need to send journal content or a full backup. Inquiry information is kept only as long as needed to handle the request; contact us below to request deletion. Your email provider’s policies apply to its processing.

This website

This site has no advertising or analytics scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Connection information processed by GitHub is subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement. Journal entries are not sent to this website.
